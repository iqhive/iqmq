    otel-tracing¶

    examples/otel-tracing — the span tree, printed: a caller's span, the publish span under it, and — after the message has crossed a broker — the process span under that, in one trace. Over the memory broker and over Redis Streams, through integrations/otel.

    The four spans, and why only two are in the tree¶

    span source parent
    publish <topic> mq.PublishMiddleware the caller's span; writes the traceparent onto the message
    process <topic> mq.Middleware the publisher's span, read out of the delivery's headers and marked remote
    receive <topic> mq.Hooks.OnReceive none — a root
    ack <topic> mq.Hooks.OnSettle none — a root

    The first two are proper children, and that is what makes one trace span the broker. The last two cannot be: mq.Hooks carries no context.Context, so there is nothing to parent them to and nothing to put them in. They are recorded with explicit timestamps and a reader sees them beside the trace. A gap in the hook surface rather than a choice, asserted by the example so that it fails the day the gap closes.

    Two smaller surprises:

    • the settle span is called ack <topic>. Its messaging.operation.name is the settlement's own — ack, nack or extend — under the pinned operation type settle. There is no settle <topic> span to search a trace viewer for.
    • there is usually more than one receive span, because a fetch loop makes as many fetches as it makes.

    Two things that silently produce nothing¶

    A bound publisher needs PublishMiddlewareFor. The specification makes a bound handle ignore Message.Topic, so a publication through one reaches the chain with an empty topic and PublishMiddleware builds a span named just publish, with no messaging.destination.name — present, plausible, impossible to group by.

    iqmq_end_to_end_duration needs PublisherConfig.Timestamp. The histogram is measured from Properties.CreatedAt, and the rule is that "Publish never generates IDs or timestamps on the hot path unless configured". Without the flag nothing stamps, the consumer has no origin, and the series is simply absent from the export — no error, no log line, and indistinguishable from a consumer that is not running.

    What it does not do¶

    It exports nothing: spans go to a tracetest.SpanRecorder and metrics to a metric.ManualReader, because the subject is the shape of the telemetry. Swapping the two providers for otlptracegrpc and a periodic reader is the whole change a service makes.

    It asserts no gauge either. iqmq_in_flight is observed at collection time and reads zero once the consumer has drained, and the lag gauge needs an Admin.Stats round trip the program does not make.

    The two arms¶

    arm driver gate
    in-process memory never skips
    broker drivers/redis IQMQ_REDIS_ADDR

    Nothing about tracing is broker-specific — what carries the trace is a header on the message — so the memory broker exercises the whole tree. What the broker arm adds is that a real broker has to carry a header it knows nothing about through its own native metadata, or the process span loses its parent and the trace breaks in two.
